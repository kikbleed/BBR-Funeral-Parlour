What's included? +

Collection of the body, coffin, hearse, tent, 2 tables, bus, death registration, graveside décor, refreshments, fresh flowers, family car, 50 chairs, 1 toilet and R120 airtime.

Groceries & cash claim options +

The grocery package includes potatoes, tomatoes, cabbage, onions, butternut, beetroot, carrots, mabele, sugar, samp, maize meal, cake flour, mala mogodu, tea bags, powdered milk, cooking oil, stew beef and braai packs. See the original flyer for quantities.

Grocery claims close on Wednesday at 10:00; the flyer states that money can be claimed afterwards. It also lists a R2,000 cash claim for the 3-tier option and R3,000 for the casket option. Terms and conditions apply — confirm the applicable claim option with our team.

Switching from another society +

No joining fee and no waiting period when switching from another society with an up-to-date policy. Bring a copy of the policy together with members' ID copies. Our team will confirm your switching eligibility.

How do I join a funeral plan? +

Contact one of our offices by phone or WhatsApp, or visit us in Lethlabile or Brits. We'll help you choose your plan and explain the documents and policy terms before you join.

Who can I include on my family plan? +

Our family plans offer a main member plus 5, 9 or 13 dependants. The listed price bands are 0–64 years and 65–74 years. Contact us to confirm how the age bands apply to your family and who is eligible.

Is there a joining fee or waiting period? +

Family plans have a R250 joining fee, or you can switch to us for free. The family flyers state a 6-month waiting period. The society package has its own switching conditions, listed in the group societies section.

What should I do when a loved one passes away? +

Please call our main office on 081 836 6788 or our Brits office on 072 365 7435. Our team can discuss the arrangements and the next steps with you.
